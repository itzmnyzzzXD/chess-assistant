The toolkit

More signal. Less waiting.

The interface keeps the most useful information close to the board and puts deeper controls one tap away.

01 / ENGINE

Stockfish + Maia

Choose tactical engine analysis or Maia's human-style move selection with on-device Core ML inference.

02 / SPEED

Fast or Deep

Fast limits search depth and candidate count. Deep keeps the full ELO-based depth for a more thorough read.

03 / REVIEW

Move quality

Track accuracy, see move grades, inspect evaluations, and copy positions or PGN for later review.

04 / BOARD

Clear at a glance

Adjust arrow count, evaluation labels, color, thickness, opacity, and the evaluation bar.

Opening repertoire

Choose your gambit.

Select an opening line in the settings panel. The built-in sequence is followed only while the game's move history matches it; once the line changes, normal engine analysis takes over.
